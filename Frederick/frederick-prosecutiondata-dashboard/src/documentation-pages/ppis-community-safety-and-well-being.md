# C﻿ommunity Safety and Well-Being

Below are a set of [Prosecutorial Performance Indicators](https://prosecutorialperformanceindicators.org/) that provide additional context about cases processed by the State's Attorney's Office (SAO). These indicators - focused on ***Community Safety and Well-Being*** - help to ensure community safety is enhanced through prosecutors’ focus on violent crimes and repeat offenders, ensuring crime victims are supported with timely outreach, and establishing prosecutors have a direct and meaningful connection with all residents.
