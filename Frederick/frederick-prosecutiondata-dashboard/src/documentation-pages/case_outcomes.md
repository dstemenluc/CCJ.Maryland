# Dispositions

This section presents data on all cases prosecuted by the State's Attorney's Office (SAO) that have reached a final disposition. Cases can be resolved in a variety of ways, including by conviction, dismissal, acquittal, Stet, probation before judgment (PBJ), or another disposition. District Court cases in which a defendant requests a jury trial receive a disposition of "jury trial prayed" in District Court and are then moved to Circuit Court. These cases are counted in the Circuit Court numbers listed below.

**Why is this important?** It is important to review how cases are resolved to ensure we are addressing serious crime effectively while being punitive only when necessary.
