# C﻿apacity and Efficiency

Below are a set of [Prosecutorial Performance Indicators](https://prosecutorialperformanceindicators.org/) that provide additional context about cases processed by the State's Attorney's Office (SAO). These indicators - focused on ***Capacity and Efficiency*** - help the SAO ensure they maximize government resources by ensuring manageable caseloads, making timely and strategic decisions about cases, and by preventing case processing delays that cause hardship for victims, witnesses and defendants, additional expenditure, and unnecessary backlogs in the court system.
